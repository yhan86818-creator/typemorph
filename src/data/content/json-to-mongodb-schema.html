MongoDB: The Leading Modern Database

MongoDB is built for developers who need flexibility and speed. While MongoDB is "schema-less" at the storage layer, production applications require a clear understanding of document structures for efficient querying and indexing. Manually defining these collection structures from high-volume JSON data is a complex task.

Streamlining NoSQL Workflows

TypeFlow simplifies your MongoDB development by inferring document schemas from your data samples:

  • Document Inference: We generate a clear representation of your expected document structure.
  • Validation Rules: Our generator provides a foundation for MongoDB's JSON Schema validation rules.
  • Fast Prototyping: Move from a raw JSON sample to a functional collection definition in milliseconds.

Private NoSQL Engineering

Your document patterns are the brain of your application. TypeFlow's local-first engine ensures your MongoDB schemas are generated locally in your browser, keeping your data patterns private while you scale your app.
