Manually mapping these into TYPESCRIPT interfaces is a recipe for a multi-million dollar disaster. One misplaced tag, one wrong data type for Tag 44 (Price), and your trading engine is toast. The Real Problem: Repeating Groups and Nested Logic

Standard converters choke on FIX Repeating Groups. They see a tag like 453 (NoPartyIDs) and have no idea how to represent the subsequent group in TYPESCRIPT. I've spent enough nights debugging 'undefined' errors in trade capture reports to know that 100% structural accuracy is non-negotiable. This tool intelligently detects group counts and generates clean, nested TYPESCRIPT arrays. No more guessing where the group ends. It handles the SOH delimiters, the checksums, and the header-body-trailer hierarchy so you don't have to. Paste your raw log, get your TYPESCRIPT, and get back to optimizing your execution logic.
