Securing Your Serverless Database

Writing Firestore rules requires a deep understanding of your data's shape and access patterns. Our tool analyzes your JSON samples and suggests allow read, write: if ... blocks that enforce data integrity and user authorization based on your object properties. This eliminates the "wide open" security risks common in early-stage development.

Local-First Privacy: Mandatory for Security Logic

Security rules define exactly who can access what in your database. Sending your data samples or rule drafts to an online converter is a massive security risk. TypeFlow Pro operates 100% locally. Your data and your security logic stay on your machine, ensuring your Firebase project's integrity is never compromised.
