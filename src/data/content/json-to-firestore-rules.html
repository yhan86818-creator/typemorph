Firestore: The Realtime NoSQL Database

Google Cloud Firestore is a powerful, serverless NoSQL database that allows developers to build realtime applications with ease. One of the most critical aspects of Firestore development is defining Security Rules to protect your data. Manually mapping your collection structures to robust security rules is a task that requires deep technical knowledge.

Why TypeFlow for Firebase Engineering?

TypeFlow provides a professional solution for Firestore design:

  • Collection Mapping: We infer the structure of your collections from your data samples.
  • Rule Foundation: Our generator provides a starting point for read and write rules based on your data types.
  • Local Processing: All transformations happen in your browser, keeping your database security patterns private.

Secure Serverless Development

TypeFlow's local-first engine ensures your Firestore patterns are generated locally. Your internal system configurations remain private while you prepare your security rules.
